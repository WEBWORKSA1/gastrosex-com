A Restaurant-Worthy Date Night for Under $25

Three complete date-night menus for two that cost less than a single restaurant main, with shopping estimates.

By the Gastrosex.com editors. Updated November 2026.

Impressive doesn't have to mean expensive. Some of the most loved dishes in the world came from home kitchens making something special out of very little. Here are three complete menus for two, each for roughly $25 or less in groceries.

Costs are rough estimates based on typical North American supermarket prices and assume you already have pantry basics such as oil, salt, pepper and flour. Prices vary by region and season.

Menu 1: Roman night (about $15)

  • Main: Spaghetti Aglio e Olio (about $4)
  • Side: Little Gem Salad with Lemon-Dijon Vinaigrette (about $5)
  • Dessert: Chocolate-Dipped Strawberries (about $6–8)

Why it works: every element is about technique, not expensive ingredients.

Menu 2: Bistro classic (about $22)

  • Main: Chicken Piccata (about $11)
  • Side: Crispy Smashed Potatoes with Garlic & Herbs (about $4)
  • Dessert: Molten Chocolate Lava Cakes for Two (about $6)

Menu 3: Weekend brunch date (about $16)

  • Shakshuka for Two (about $7)
  • Lemon Ricotta Pancakes (about $6)
  • Fresh orange juice and good coffee (about $3)

Where to spend and where to save

  • Spend on finishing ingredients you taste directly: good olive oil, real Parmesan, good chocolate.
  • Save on things that are cooked down: onions, canned tomatoes, cooking wine (never cook with a wine you wouldn't drink, but it needn't be expensive).
  • Atmosphere is almost free: candles, a clean tablecloth and a playlist cost little and change everything.
