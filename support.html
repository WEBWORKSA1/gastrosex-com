Support the kitchen

Gastrosex.com is free, independent and paid for by ads and readers like you. Your support goes into recipe testing, contests and prizes, promoting the site, and paying the cooks, photographers and writers who make it.

Where your money goes

  • 35% Ongoing operations: hosting, tools, ingredients for recipe testing
  • 30% Hiring talent: freelance recipe developers, photographers, video creators
  • 20% Promotion and marketing to reach more home cooks
  • 15% Contests and prizes for our community

We publish a short summary of support received and how it was spent each quarter on this page.

Other ways to help

  • Share a recipe you loved with a friend
  • Enter the monthly contest
  • Send a correction or a recipe idea
  • Turn off your ad blocker on this site
