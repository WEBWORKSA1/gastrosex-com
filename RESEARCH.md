# Gastrosex.com — Research & Strategy (November 2026)

## 1. What the domain means
"Gastrosexual" comes from the 2008 report *The Emergence of the Gastrosexual* by the London trend agency the Future Foundation, sponsored by the food brand PurAsia. It described (mainly male) cooks aged 25–44 who cook for pleasure and for the praise of others. The coverage reported that about half of people say cooking ability makes someone more attractive and nearly a quarter of men aged 18–34 cook partly to impress a partner (sources: FSR Magazine; The Food Channel, 2008).

**Concept chosen:** a family-safe *cook-to-impress* food hub: date-night recipes for two, full-evening planning tools, and a lead funnel for private chefs, couples cooking classes and custom menus.

## 2. Risks and mitigations
| Risk | Why it matters | Mitigation built in |
|---|---|---|
| "sex" in the domain | AdSense allows family-safe content only; sexual or suggestive text, images or advice are restricted or prohibited | Food-only content, no innuendo in titles, descriptive "gastrosexual" origin explained on About and in a guide, editorial policy with a family-friendly standard, moderated user submissions |
| Low AdSense RPM in food | AdSense food RPMs are typically $1–5; premium networks pay far more | Lead gen + newsletter as primary revenue; site built to qualify for Mediavine (50k sessions) / Raptive (100k pageviews) |
| No photography | Recipe sites usually rely on photos | Typographic "menu" design, embedded technique videos; photographer role listed on Careers |
| Leads need fulfilment | Promising a 24-hour reply requires someone to act | Leads land in the owner inbox; partner programme + affiliate fallbacks (Phase 12) |
| Trademark | Avoid implying affiliation with the term's originators | Descriptive use, no rights claimed, explicit non-affiliation and takedown route |

## 3. Revenue model (planning numbers)
- **Display ads.** AdSense ~$1–5 RPM; Mediavine ~$15–35; Raptive ~$20–50 (industry guides, 2025–26). At 100k pageviews/month: about $200–500 on AdSense versus $1,500–3,500 on Mediavine.
- **Leads.** Couples cooking classes sell at about $70–110 a person (Cozymeal, CourseHorse listings); private-chef dinners for two run about $180–550+ in total. *Assumption for planning:* a 10% referral fee and 20% of requests booking gives roughly $4–11 per plan request, more for proposal/anniversary dinners.
- **Newsletter sponsorships.** Food lists of 5k–20k engaged subscribers sell about $200–2,000 per send (industry guide, 2026).
- **Also:** sponsored recipes/tools, contest prize sponsors, partner listings, reader support, and outright sale of the site/domain via web.works/contact.

### Gaps nobody fills (our differentiation)
1. A menu planner that outputs the *whole evening*: menu, shopping list, minute-by-minute timeline, drinks and setting.
2. Recipes written for two, with "why it impresses", plating and make-ahead notes as standard.
3. A direct bridge from "I'll cook" to "have someone cook for us" (lead funnel) inside every recipe.
