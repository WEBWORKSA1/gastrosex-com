Questions

Is this free?

Yes. Sending a request costs nothing and doesn't commit you to anything. If you book, you pay the chef or school directly at their price.

How much does a private chef for two cost?

For a three- or four-course dinner with standard ingredients, expect roughly $90–180 a person, including shopping, cooking, service and cleaning up. Premium menus with wine pairing can be $250 a person or more.

Which cities do you cover?

Send your request wherever you are. Availability is strongest in major cities in North America, the UK, Europe and Australia, and online classes work everywhere.

What happens to my details?

We use them only to answer your request. With your permission we share the request (not your payment details) with chefs or schools so they can quote. Read our privacy policy.
