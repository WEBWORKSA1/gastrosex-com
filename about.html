About Gastrosex.com

Food that says you made an effort.

Gastrosex.com is an independent food publication for anyone who cooks to impress: the person planning a first dinner at home, the partner surprising someone on an anniversary, the friend who loves hosting. Our recipes serve two, our tools plan the whole evening, and our guides cover everything around the plate.

Where the name comes from

"Gastrosexual" was coined in 2008 consumer research by the Future Foundation to describe people who cook for pleasure and for the people they love, rather than out of obligation. We use it in its broad, modern sense: anyone who says "I made an effort for you" with food. Read the full story in What is a gastrosexual?

What we publish

  • Recipes for two that scale, with timings, make-ahead plans, plating notes and drink pairings.
  • Guides on technique, timing, presentation, pairing and hosting.
  • Free tools that turn ideas into a menu, a shopping list and a timeline.
  • A monthly contest that celebrates readers' plates.

Family-friendly, always

This is a food site. Our content is written for a general audience and focuses on cooking, hospitality and the joy of sharing a meal.

How we're funded

We're supported by display advertising (Google AdSense), reader contributions and clearly labeled sponsorships. Advertisers never influence our recipes or recommendations. See our editorial policy.

Get in touch

Corrections, ideas and questions are welcome through our contact page. If you're interested in this website, the domain name, sponsorship, advertising or partnership, use the owner contact page.
